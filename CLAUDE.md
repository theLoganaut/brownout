# Project Brownout

A non-commercial hobby battle royale: Blackout's (Black Ops 4) gunplay and feel, faster movement, our own 1 km map, POIs that change with electricity. Built by modifying Photon Fusion 2's **BR200** sample (fresh Unity Asset Store import).
